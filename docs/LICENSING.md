## What you can do today

- **Read, copy, modify, fork, and redistribute** the source.
- **Run it in non-production settings** — evaluation, development, testing,
  demos, internal experiments, research.
- Contribute changes back (your contributions are licensed under the same
  terms).

## What needs a commercial licence today

The Additional Use Grant is **None**, so **any production use** — running the
software to intake, audit, or book real deals for your organization or for
customers — requires a commercial licence from ElcanoTek until the Change
Date. Write to **licensing@elcanotek.com**.

"Production" is read the ordinary way: if the deals it books are real, it is
production.

## What happens on the Change Date

On **2028-09-02** (or four years after a given version's first public
release, whichever comes first) this version of Deal Onboarding automatically
becomes available under the **MIT License**, with no action from anyone.
Each later release carries its own Change Date, stated in its `LICENSE`.
